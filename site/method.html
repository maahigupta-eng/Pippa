The method

Pippa follows Quo Vadis, a method for resource mobilisation developed by Andras Vamos-Goldman. Five moves, always in this order.

  1. 1

    Frame the case

    What exactly are you solving, and how? Who is affected, who could help, who might resist? Why would anyone back this approach?

  2. 2

    Before the beginning

    Where does the need sit in your goals and your structure? Could it scale? Plan for that early, because it shapes the ask.

  3. 3

    Look inward first

    Office, then region, then the whole organisation. Internal resources rarely cover everything. They cut the ask and give you something to offer.

    HumanSkills staff already have that others would pay for.
    MaterialBuildings, vehicles, land and equipment with spare capacity.
    ProjectRoom in running projects, like a clinic or a school.
    SavingCosts you can cut or share with a partner.
  4. 4

    Then look outward

    Ask for a range, never one number. Pick the form that fits: grant, loan, in-kind or partnership. Decide whose money you'll accept. Say plainly what the funder gains.

  5. 5

    Think about the system

    Could this project change what causes the need in the first place? Say so only when it's real. One line is enough.
