Privacy

Pippa holds only what it needs, never sells your data, and never uses your material to train AI models.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. If you request access, we receive your name, email, organisation and message by email, through our form provider, Web3Forms. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them if you don't go on to use Pippa.

Using Pippa

When you sign in, Pippa stores your email address, the case you set up, your answers to its questions, and the results it gives you, so you can come back to them. It keeps a daily count of your runs for fair-use limits, and a record that you accepted its terms.

Files you upload are not stored. They are read in your browser or sent once to our AI provider for the analysis you asked for. Only the result is saved. Questions you ask Pippa about a result are not stored either.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare hosts Pippa, stores your saved work and runs sign-in. Anthropic provides the AI model; under its commercial terms, material sent through its API is not used to train its models. Both are based in the United States. We don't sell your data or share your work with anyone else unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have access to Pippa, and delete it within 30 days after. You can delete individual results, or everything at once, from inside Pippa at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. Write to maahigupta@gmail.com. If you are in the UK, the EU or Switzerland, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Please don't upload personal data about others

Remove names and details that identify beneficiaries, children, families or staff before you upload a document.
