How it works

Pippa reads what you bring, weighs it against what funders look for, and returns a short, sourced brief. You stay in charge of every decision.

1

Frame the case

Your case

A structured brief on who you are, what you want to change and what you need. Every analysis reads from it, so the advice fits an organisation, a research group or a single applicant.

You give
A few short answers
You get
Advice fitted to you
2

Look inward first

Internal resources

Registers, budgets and reports, read for capacity that is idle, underused or shareable. Each opportunity is weighed for mission risk, effort and confidence.

You give
A register, budget or CV
You get
Ranked opportunities, with sources
3

Then look outward

Funder check

A funder's own guidelines and past grants, read against your case. Pippa tests eligibility, scores the fit, names the way in and sets out what the partnership offers them.

You give
The funder's own materials
You get
A verdict, a route in, an ask
4

On call

Ask Pippa

Press on any finding, test a framing, or ask what would change the verdict. Answers stay grounded in your documents.

You give
A question
You get
A straight answer
