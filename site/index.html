Read the books before you knock on doors.

Pippa reads your asset registers, budgets and funders’ own materials. It finds the capacity you’ve stopped seeing, tells you plainly which funders are worth your time, and shows you how to pitch what you already have.

Request access See how it works

Official development aid fell by roughly $50 billion in 2025, a drop of 23% in a single year. Philanthropy can’t replace it, and the funders who remain want partners who bring something to the table.

Source: OECD preliminary figures, reported by Fortune, 2026.

Three steps, in the order good fundraisers already work.

  1. 1

    Frame the case

    The problem, the mission, who’s involved and why your approach works. Set it once; Pippa reads it before every analysis.

  2. 2

    Look inward

    Upload an asset register, budget or programme report. Pippa finds idle buildings, equipment, staff time and money, and flags what must not be touched.

  3. 3

    Look outward

    Add a funder’s own materials. Pippa checks eligibility, scores the fit, names the best route in, and says what you can offer them.

A verdict you can act on, in plain language.

No twenty-page report. Pippa returns a short brief: a clear verdict, the reasons, what to bring, what to watch out for, and one next step with an owner. Then you can ask it questions about the result.

  • Every claim has a source. Findings point to the line, row or page they came from. Missing facts are listed as missing.

Built to be trusted with real organisations.

Works only from what you give it
Pippa doesn’t browse or guess. If a fact isn’t in your documents or answers, it says so.
Asks before it answers
Two to four sharp questions about the gaps that would change the verdict. You can skip them.
Keeps your material private
Uploaded files aren’t stored, and nothing you share is used to train AI models.

Born in a classroom of people who raise money for a living.

Pippa grew out of a course on resource mobilisation for senior leaders of a global child-welfare network: national directors with decades of fundraising behind them. It runs the method they practised by hand, faster, and on their own documents.
